MOD1 is a simple 5 Channel mono passive mixer.



Jacks 1, 2, A, 3 & 4 are considered the inputs, and B is the sum output.

On the PCB each input is connected to the B output via a 4.7K resistor.

Since it is passive, is normal to expect a decreased volume.
